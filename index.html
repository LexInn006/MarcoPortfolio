About me

I'm a Computer Science student majoring in Multimedia at BINUS University, based in Indonesia. I'm drawn to the space where design meets building — figuring out what people actually need, shaping it into a clear interface, and then making it work as a real app.

My projects reflect that loop: I've worked in a team to design and build a civic-reporting app, and taken a children's learning concept from user interviews all the way to a full Figma prototype. I enjoy every step — talking to users, designing flows, and turning them into something that runs.

Outside of coursework, I've volunteered teaching kids the basics of Canva.
